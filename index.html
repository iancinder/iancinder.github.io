Aug 2026 to nowTexas Guadaloop, levitation team

Maglev pod digital twin and cascaded control

I build the Python and MuJoCo simulation of our two-yoke maglev test rig. The motor driver, inductive gap sensors, current sensors, and Hall-effect sensors are all modeled so the simulated controller only sees the signals the real firmware sees. The goal is a sim that is honest enough to tune controllers on before they touch hardware.

  • Designed a cascaded loop (outer PID on gap, Hall-feedback force loop, 4.8 kHz PI current loop) that cut post-disturbance gap ringing from 1.4 mm to 0.03 mm and peak deviation by 40% in simulation.
  • Validated the polynomial force model against 4,471 Ansys FEA points (0.19 N RMSE) and corrected the pod’s mass and equilibrium model, removing 4.35 A per coil of holding current.
  • Added a parametric step-target setpoint generator and robustness tests for parameter error, coil heating, and supply sag.
Jan to May 2026University of Oklahoma, School of ECE

Edge AI for EV-induced voltage sags

First-author paper accepted at IEEE TPEC 2027, with Dr. Paul Moses. Uncoordinated EV charging can drag a distribution feeder below safe voltage faster than a centralized controller can respond. I trained a Random Forest on thousands of simulated grid scenarios, ported it to a TI C2000 microcontroller, and used it as a predictive controller that throttles charging current through PWM instead of cutting it off.

  • Model accuracy of R² = 0.977 with 0.0017 p.u. mean absolute error on held-out data.
  • Runs on the C2000 in 177 µs using 65 KB of flash, a quarter of the chip.
  • In processor-in-the-loop tests, held voltage at the 0.95 p.u. ANSI limit while the unmitigated grid collapsed.
